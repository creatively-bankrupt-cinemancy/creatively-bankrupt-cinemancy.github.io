Cinemancy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Cinemancy is a game made by Creatively Bankrupt ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game handles. Questions: creativelybankruptcinemancy@gmail.com.

What we collect

We don't ask for your name, email address or any account, and we don't run our own servers that store your data. Your progress is saved on your device.

Advertising

The Android version can show optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (from your IP address), how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information about app performance. Google handles this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Online co-op

Online games joined with a code use Epic Online Services. It creates an anonymous ID for your device and relays game traffic between the players in your game. Epic handles this under its privacy policy. Local (LAN) games send data only between devices on your own network.

Security

Data sent by the ad and online services above is encrypted in transit.

Children

Cinemancy is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your choices

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete. You can limit ad-related data by resetting or deleting your advertising ID, by not watching rewarded ads, or by uninstalling the game. For data held by Google or Epic, use their tools linked above.

Changes

If the game starts collecting anything new, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
